Information

Greenfield Map for Minecraft is a city map created by TheJestr. Greenfield is a city like no other. Built to the finest detail, but also on a carefully planned large scale. The city is built using a 1:1 Scale (1 block = 1 cubic meter) and is made to feel like you are walking around a real city whilst you are exploring it. Greenfield started back in August 2011 and is still being worked on to this day to bring new updates and new areas to explore. The things that you can find in Greenfield are the same as any large real life city. There is a Downtown area with huge skyscrapers, nice rich suburbs, poor decaying ghettos, industrial areas, as well as a vast network of roads, highways, railways, and subways. The map is also littered with secrets and Easter eggs for you to find if you look hard enough.
